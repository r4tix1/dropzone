Dropzone 1.0.0

How to play

  • Fight 23 bots. The last one standing wins.
  • You start with a pistol. Walk over loot to pick it up. Colored beams mark where it is.
  • Stay inside the purple storm circle (see the minimap). It shrinks in phases and hurts more each time.
  • Reload with R. An empty magazine reloads automatically when you fire. Picking up a gun you already own, or an ammo box, adds reserve ammo.

Damage numbers

  • White: health damage   Blue: shield damage   Yellow: headshot (1.5x damage)
  • A blue ring pops when you break an enemy's shield.

Loot colors

  • Red: health   Blue: shield   White: ammo
  • Yellow: rifle   Orange: shotgun   Light blue: SMG   Green: sniper

Difficulty

Changes how accurately the bots aim. You can switch it any time.
